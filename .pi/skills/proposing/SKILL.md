name: proposing
description: The method that should be taken when making proposals on during devleopment planning.
---

Start by gathering all the contect you can to give an informed proposals for the users request. The create a full list of proposals that you think will implement complete the request. Give them to the user one by on so you can discuss with them. Discuss until they either approve, reject or help you amend your proposal. After the decision on a proposal has been made you should do a review of what you have learned and assess the previously approved proposal and pending ones to look for required ammendments and redundencies before moving on to the next. Once all decisions have been made and there are no more pending proposals you should give a summary of what has been decided before moving on.
